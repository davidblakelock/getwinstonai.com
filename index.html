A different relationship

No ads. No data selling.
Ever.

Winston has one customer: you. Our business model is a subscription, not advertising — which means there's no incentive to monetize your attention or sell what Winston learns about you.

Zero ads

Winston is completely ad-free. No advertisers. No promoted content. No one paying to influence what Winston tells you.

Zero data selling

Your data is never sold to data brokers, advertisers, or any third party for any commercial purpose. Not now. Not ever. Not in the fine print.

Minimum collected

When your data has no commercial value to us, we have no reason to collect more than we need. Winston collects the minimum required to be genuinely useful to you.

An idea worth borrowing

An old idea
for a very modern problem.

The Stoics wrote what amounts to an operating system for how to live — focus on what you control, act with intention, let the rest go. It's having a real resurgence right now, especially among people who never expected to care about two-thousand-year-old philosophy. Winston is quietly built on that same idea — not as a lesson, just a reminder that attention is finite and not everything deserves it. You'll notice it in the Morning Run Down, the Evening Wrap Up, and the occasional question worth actually sitting with. Winston isn't just interested in whether you got everything done. It's interested in whether the things you did actually mattered.
